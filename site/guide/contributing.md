# Add your mod

o-mods is open source, and every mod here started as an idea someone wrote down. You do not need a big plan. A small, useful mod is a good mod. Every change goes through one issue and one review, so the collection stays tidy.

## How it works

1. Open an issue. Tell us what your mod does and why it helps.
2. Send a pull request. Add your mod folder with a README and a demo.
3. Get a review. A maintainer reads it, may ask for changes, and then merges it.
